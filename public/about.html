Spatial capture for the real world

One team,
one legacy.

While working with emerging technologies in 3D media production, IRL Scanning & Modeling co-founder Lain McGregor recognized an opportunity not only to help usher in a revolution in how we capture, preserve, and interact with the world, but also to ensure the coming age of technology is guided toward a humanity-focused future.

With this vision in mind, a small, talented team was assembled to demonstrate two things: first, that high fidelity, universally accessible, and resource-efficient digital twins could have meaningful value across virtually every industry; and second, that technological progress need not isolate and confound us as humans, but can instead preserve and empower culture, enrich our shared experiences, and ultimately bring us closer together.
